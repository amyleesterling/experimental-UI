Adjusting

Adjust one thing, see the whole picture

A settings panel exists to change what is behind it, and while you are changing it the panel is the one thing you should not be looking at. Take hold of any control here and every other control leaves. What stays is the one you are holding, at its own place, over the scene it is changing, with its value live. Let go and the panel comes back. It works the same for a switch as for a slider, and from the keyboard. The scene is generated, a stand in for whatever a real panel would change.
